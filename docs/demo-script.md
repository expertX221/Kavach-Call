# Three-minute judge demo

1. **0:00-0:30 — Problem:** "A familiar voice can pressure you into sending money. Kavach helps you pause and verify." Open the default verification screen. Point out the simulated-session label and explained indicators.
2. **0:30-1:10 — Detect:** Open Practice studio and choose the CBI/digital-arrest example. Watch phrases arrive and multiple signals stack into a high-risk warning. Explain that this is a local heuristic, not a fraud probability or legal verdict.
3. **1:10-1:50 — Verify:** Choose the family-joke example or the family question method. Show that "just joking" does not auto-clear a warning. Independently check, then acknowledge dismissal. New speech remains subject to analysis.
4. **1:50-2:20 — Report:** Create a practice report draft. Show that excerpts are optional and require manual redaction review. Download the JSON. No report or family message has been sent.
5. **2:20-2:45 — Inclusion:** Demonstrate low-quality transcript handling and large-text mode. Mention the limited Hindi/Tamil examples without claiming measured multilingual accuracy.
6. **2:45-3:00 — Business:** Propose a free practice/verification tier, a future paid family plan, and a consent-based bank/telecom integration. These are business hypotheses, not existing revenue or partnerships.

For microphone demos, use consenting speakers and a supported browser. Start on-device-only mode first. If unavailable, use typed text or separately consent to provider transcription. Do not imply a microphone is intercepting a normal phone call.

Track real, consented pilot evidence during the traction period: completed practice sessions, verification completion, qualitative feedback, false positives on labeled samples and willingness to pay. Do not manufacture usage, accuracy or customer numbers.
